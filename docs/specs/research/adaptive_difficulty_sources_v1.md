### 1.2 Verification (done by this stage, not taken from the workers)

Every source was opened at its primary bibliographic record and checked for **title and first-author surname**:
- **DOIs:** the Crossref registry record (`api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`), or DOI content negotiation where Crossref lacked the record;
- **arXiv:** the abstract page's citation metadata;
- **proceedings and journal pages:** PMLR, NeurIPS, ACL Anthology, JEDM, the EDM 2026 proceedings, JTLA and Cambridge Core, via the page's citation metadata;
- **PDFs:** the paper itself, by text extraction of the first two pages, for EDM 2013, the Bjork chapter, IJCAI 2013, the ERIC copy of EDM 2016 and the PAP 2018 workshop;
- **software:** the GitHub repository.

**Results:**
- **121 of 121 candidate sources verified.**
- **Three matched by hand.** The record and expected titles differed only by a Unicode hyphen or `<scp>` markup in the Crossref title: `[ban2001]`, `[liu2025]`, `[schmidli2014]`.
- **Two corrected.**
  - Worker (c)'s DOI for `[embretson1999]` (`10.1007/BF02294568`) resolves to a different Psychometrika paper (Arminger, Stein & Wittenberg, "Mixtures of Conditional Mean- and Covariance-Structure Models"). A Crossref bibliographic search found the right one, `10.1007/BF02294564`, which is the DOI used here.
  - The Harvard Data Science Review page for `[schmid2022]` refused automated access (HTTP 403). The source is verified by its DOI `10.1162/99608f92.3f1772ce`.
- **Two that workers flagged UNVERIFIED are verified here** through their Crossref records: `[lesmes2010]` (worker a) and `[bi2024]` (worker e).
- **Dropped: none.** Every candidate resolved to a matching primary record.
- **Added 2026-10-15: 5 of 5 verified** by the same method: `[adams1997]`, `[chen2015q]`, `[culpepper2019]` and `[hoeting1999]` at their Crossref records, `[capstick2024]` at its arXiv abstract page. - **The year column** is the primary record's date (online-first issue, or the first arXiv version), so it can differ from the year in a key. **The venue** is what the record shows. Later journal or conference publication claimed by a worker is not asserted unless the record carries it.

## 2. Synthesis

The synthesis is in the spec (§4.1, R1–R12, and the item-by-item verdict on the agreed direction in §4.2). In one paragraph:
- **Targets and objectives.** Target the expected response at a feature point, not a point difficulty (`[sinharay2003]`, `[deboeck2008]`). Give practice and probes different objectives (`[chaloner1995]`, `[cavagnaro2010]`). Calibrate by psi-marginal information within acceptable difficulty (`[prins2013]`).
- **Dynamics.** Make the learner state dynamic (`[zhang2019]`, `[wang2013]`, `[glickman1999]`).
- **Outside estimates.** Treat LLM labels and solver panels as item-side signals with learned weights (`[an2026]`, `[li2025]`, `[acquaye2026]`, `[isophys2026]`). Treat history-conditioned forecasts as competing experts scored by proper rules (`[gneiting2007]`, `[choe2023]`, `[herbster1998]`).
- **The band and the limits.** Keep 70–80% as an engineering default to be tested on delayed outcomes (`[wilson2019]`, `[klinkenberg2011]`, `[papousek2016]`). Be explicit about what one learner's data cannot identify (`[chen2021]`, `[fu2016]`, `[bolsinova2025]`).
- **Pooling.** Pool later with robust priors and learner-level privacy (`[schmidli2014]`, `[liu2020]`, `[steiner2018]`).
- **Relatedness (added 2026-10-15).** Let an expert, here an LLM, propose which topics an item draws on and which are related, and learn from the data how far to trust it (`[culpepper2019]`, `[capstick2024]`, `[adams1997]`, `[chen2015q]`), averaging over hyperparameters rather than fixing them (`[hoeting1999]`).

Each worker's open questions are folded into the spec's §16 (deferrals with triggers) and §17 (open questions).

- The vaults were loaded with `load_vault(..., load_environment=False)`, so no settings or key files were read. The databases were opened read-only. The output is aggregates only.

**Method.**
- Items: counts by `difficulty_source`, `capability`, presence of `task_features`, `expected_minutes` and `discrimination_profiles`.
- Attempts: graded attempts other than `self_report`, distinct items, items attempted once vs at least twice, and the shares that are primed, hinted, timed and confidence-tapped.
- **Label vs outcome:** for each item, the first graded attempt that was neither primed nor hinted, set against the item's `difficulty` where `difficulty_source = "llm_estimate"`:
  - Spearman rank correlation with average ranks for ties;
  - a within-capability residual correlation (Pearson on values centred within each capability);
  - approximate 95% intervals by the Fisher z transform (`se = 1/√(n−3)`, and `1/√(n−3−(groups−1))` within capability).
