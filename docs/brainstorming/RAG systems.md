We could couple [[Knowledge Tracing]] with retrieval requests. We can retrieve concept level pedagogical knowledge and instance level student error cases. 
- explain this from my textbook
- give me an alternative explanation
- show me a geometric version
- find a worked example (in our canonical sources)
- remind me what i previously asked about this
Retrieval is match the incoming error against instances, draft preliminary response, then use that draft to retrieve concept level pedagogical knowledge that grounds and corrects it. Although this might not be entirely useful for grounding for world knowledge, perhaps its more grounded for maybe "how do learners in general fail here" (which may already be superseded by the backbone LLM) + local learner traces.
