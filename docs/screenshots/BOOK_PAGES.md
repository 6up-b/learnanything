# Screenshots that show a page of a book (storage S0, D19)

`docs/specs/spec_storage_sync_v1.md` §3.5 and D19: screenshots of the app are
the project's own work and stay, but a screenshot that shows a page of a book
may be published only as that book's licence allows. Every tracked screenshot
under `docs/` (88 PNG files on 2026-10-25) was looked at for S0. This is the
complete list of those that show a book or paper page, with what was done.
A guard test cannot see what an image shows, so a new screenshot of a book
page is added to this list by whoever adds it.
