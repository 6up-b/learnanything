# Refactor B8: the rest, by value

Step B8 of `docs/specs/refactor_strategy_v3.md` (§2.4, arc B; §3.8's last rows), plus the leftovers B3-B7 handed on in their open items. Branch `refactor/b8-rest-by-value`, based on main `4faa750dd9` (B1-B7 landed).

**Result.** Each remaining boundary was read first. Where a real question had no stored answer, it now leaves a decision record. That covers a plan logger that raised, a shared daily cap's refusal, a lesson closed by its budget, the curriculum path's daily re-judgment, an AD commission job that generated nothing, a figure prediction's gap reading stopped by the figure gate, and the FSRS review behind a grade. The rest are written down in ADR-017's new table, each with the rows that already answer it or the reason a record isn't worth it. With B2-B7 that makes the inventory complete, with no silent gap (§3.8, updated). Nothing served changes. No migration, no successor tag.

## Step 0: what was read, and the verdict

Three read-only passes covered the scope, and I checked the sites that got records myself. The verdict for every boundary is in ADR-017 ("B8: boundaries with no record, and why not"). In short:

- **Already answerable:** teacher turns, checks and closes (`lesson_turns`, `lesson_receipts`); Reader retrieval (`question_events.source_context.source_retrieval`, `find_practice`'s receipt signals); re-read carry-over (`source_span_reanchors`, the import job's `carried_over`); the curriculum path's choice (path rows, ledger, the plan's `split_json.reading_path`); retries (a child receipt per physical attempt); and most spend catchers (follow-up generation, AD forecasts, Reader intent, quick check, follow-ups, hint writer, grading).
- **Not worth a record:** alias resolution, which is pure and recomputes; display-only cues; learner-initiated selectors (quick check, `find_practice`); the TUI's probe door (C7 rebuilds it); `chooser_enabled = false` and tied judgments (B5's view, confirmed).
- **Silent, now recorded:** the seven below.
- **Silent and left open, with the reason:** the AI route's fallback. Routing holds no connection and runs inside router paths with a time budget, so a record there could wait on the write lock (B1's rule). The fix is a nullable receipt column with its own migration. Also left open: ingest's primary extractor failure class, the Quick Add scope fallback, the teacher brief's swallowed reads, and rich figure attempts' problem codes.

## The rules from B1 and the strategy

- **A record never changes a decision.** Every write is `provenance.write`'s savepoint, or `write_decision`, or `refusal_records.write`/`write_on`, and none of them raises. Each builder runs inside its writer or is wrapped (`_fsrs_review_summary` returns None on any error). The curriculum wrapper returns the job's own dict, or re-raises its own exception. The envelope raises the same refusal after the record. Tests cover the parts most likely to break this: a cap record that can't be built still refuses, and a non-finite FSRS number is dropped while the grade record stays.
- **Router paths never wait on the write lock.** The logger's record joins the block's transaction. The envelope's record joins the reservation's `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, which already holds the lock. The lesson, the curriculum refresh and the commission job write after their decision, off the router's path, in a background job or a learner's lesson action.
- **AD operations.** No B8 writer runs inside an `ad_operation`. The commission job writes after its operation closes. AD's envelope opts out. `commission_point`'s denial inside generation runs in the caller's AD view, so it stays on the JSONL (ADR-017).
- **The replay closure.** `source_retrieval.deliverable_path` is in it (the closure reaches it for the path's reads), so `test_decision_records_is_unreachable_from_replay` widens `_RECORD_WRITERS` on purpose. `test_a_rebuild_never_calls_the_record_and_keeps_its_rows` counts `_write_refresh_record` during a live rebuild of the head fixture and finds zero. `write_decision` is also refused outright there. The other writers reach the table through `refusal_records` (already declared), `grade_records` (declared) or modules outside the closure (`teacher.service`, `content.pipeline.jobs`). `adaptive_difficulty.spend`'s comment was worded so that it doesn't name the table, which the scan checks.
- **No prose.** Ids that aren't codes become `sha256:<16 hex>`. A curriculum reason that is a sentence is dropped. The prose test plants learner text in every new builder, as ids, facts, a logger name, an owner kind and an exception message.
- **AD23's contract** compares every new row of every table between a copy with protected material and one with it absent. `attempt_surprise` and `practice_item_state` sit outside that comparison as the incumbent's own state, since the incumbent counts protected answers by design. The grade's `facts.fsrs` copies exactly that state, so it differed between the copies. `tests/test_ad23_contract.py`'s normalisation now sets `facts.fsrs` aside by name on `grade` records only, with the reason in its docstring. Every other field of every record is still compared, and the guard and its lists are unchanged. **This touches a firewall test, so it's flagged here for the owner.** The alternative was to keep the FSRS review off the record and leave "why is this due then?" unanswered.

## Proofs

**P-tests** (`tests/test_remaining_decision_records.py`, 14 tests). Every model is a fake. Every record is read back through `learnloop why --json` or `provenance.read`, and every `why` output is checked for a planted string.

- **The plan logger.** An exploding logger on the mixture env: the plan still serves an item, and `why <trace>` and `why block:<id>` give `router_decision_row`/`failed`/`logger_error` with `RuntimeError` at `tests.test_remaining_decision_records:exploding:<line>`, `decision_kind`, `logger` and `applied`. The message appears nowhere. An exploding `log_plan_interrupt_decision` on G4's follow-up env gives the same, with `decision_kind = plan_interrupt`, and no ledger row (as before).
- **The shared cap.** `SharedDailyEnvelope` at 0.01 refuses a 0.5 request. The message is unchanged; `rule`, `limit`, `used`, `requested` and `scope` are set; and one `spend_admission`/`envelope_cap` record carries `limit_usd`, `request_usd`, `spent_usd` and `scope`. An admitted request writes none. A record whose builder raises still refuses, and logs no message. AD's envelope has `record_refusals=False`.
- **The lesson.** TE1's metered teacher at 1.5 times a request's reservation closes the lesson with `budget`, and its record names `lesson_envelope`, `max_estimated_usd`, the lesson, `purpose = teacher_turn`, the limit, and an estimate that passes it.
- **The curriculum refresh.** Five outcomes through `refresh_for_plan` give three records (`refused`/`plan_only`, `deferred`/`coalesced` with the goal, depth and state key, and `excluded`/`switched_off` with the sentence dropped), none for `none` or `fresh`, and the job's dicts are returned unchanged. A job that raises is re-raised, with a `failed` record.
- **The commission job.** Staged as closed, view unavailable or guard denied (`GuardDenied`), it gives three records naming each case under `job:<id>`, while the job's result stays "commission no longer open".
- **The text-gap job's gate.** Figures switched off after the first request: the job still releases (`retry_later`, `figures_changed`), and one `figure_admission`/`refused`/`figures_off` record exists.
- **The FSRS review.** Two real submissions three days apart on the live-shape vault. Each grade record carries `facts.fsrs` with every field; the second's `elapsed_days` is 3.0, and its `prior_stability` and `prior_due_at` equal the first's `stability` and `due_at`. A non-finite number and a prose value are dropped, and the grade record still passes the prose check.
- **No prose.** Every new builder, given planted learner text as ids, facts, a logger, an owner kind and an exception message, passes `check_no_prose`, and the text is absent.

## Storage

The new rows are rare. A logger failure, a cap refusal, a lesson's budget close, one refresh outcome a day, and a skipped commission job each write one record of a few hundred bytes. `facts.fsrs` adds about 400 bytes to each live grade record, one per attempt, against B6's slates at about 30 rows per attempt cycle.
