**Router and AD outcomes after an attempt (§2.3's open item).** Followed through: neither has a live writer. `router_outcomes` is DERIVED and written only by its replayer (`rebuild_orchestrator.py:_run_router_outcomes`, `router/outcomes.py:rebuild_router_outcomes`, which replaces it whole); live readers (`daily_plan/router_retrieval.py:162`, `ops/capture_report.py`, `cli/router.py`) derive outcomes at read time with `derive_router_outcomes`. `ad_state` and `ad_attempt_trace` are DERIVED and written only by AD's replayer (`adaptive_difficulty/replay.py:rebuild`, from the rebuild and `ad_successor_upgrade`); AD serving reads the attempt ledger at serve time and its selection is the `ad_selections` row of the next slot. So an attempt writes no router or AD outcome, and there is nothing to record there: the next plan decision's records (B2) and its AD selection carry what the attempt changed.

**The queue hop (B1's open item `job_enqueued`).** Four queues carry the chain: `post_attempt_jobs` (diagnose, embed; enqueued by `followups.enqueue_post_attempt_diagnosis` from the post-attempt pipeline, by `insert_blind_grade_record` on a regrade, by `enqueue_embed_job` after a diagnosis), `learner_text_gap_jobs` (`db/stores/learner_gaps.enqueue_job`, after a diagnosis and from a dozen text sources), and `followup_generation_jobs` (`db/stores/followup_serve.enqueue_generation_job`, and `requeue_generation_job` for IH2's re-decision). Each enqueue returns the new job's id only when the row is new. The sidecar's worker leases inside each queue's `process_next_*`, so the job's id is known only after the lease, inside the processor.

**The trace across the queue.** `provenance` gains `continue_trace(trace_id, cause=)` and `current_cause()`: `trace_scope` now also remembers the trace it opened with and resets a cause, and `continue_trace` switches the innermost scope's trace in place (its exit restores what came before; outside any scope it changes nothing). Each queue's processor (`split.jobs.process_next_job`, `text_gaps.jobs.process_next_gap_job`, `followup_run.jobs.process_next_job`) calls `db.stores.provenance.continue_job_trace(repository, job_id)` right after its lease: it reads the newest `job_enqueued` record's trace and id for that job and continues them. The post-attempt worker keeps one scope per job (B1), so a job's trace is its enqueuer's and each job is scoped apart; a job with no record keeps the scope's own. The B1 open item asked for the enqueuer's trace "as parent": the job's records take the `job_enqueued` record as `parent_id`, and the job continues its trace, as §3.3 and ADR-017 describe.

## Proofs

**P-tests** (`tests/test_attempt_chain_records.py`, 18 tests; every model a fake, read back through `learnloop why --json`, the answer text checked absent from every output):

- The chain: a blind-graded miss submitted under `TRACE_SUBMIT`, then the worker's diagnose and follow-up slots run under a different RPC's trace. `attempt:<id>` gives one `grade` (trace `TRACE_SUBMIT`, `blind_grader`, the verdict, score, maximum, prompt and algorithm versions, `detail = practice_attempts`). The trace gives the diagnose job's `job_enqueued`, the `diagnosis` (`completed`) whose `parent_id` is that record, the workflow's generation job queued by the diagnosis (parent the diagnose job's record), and the diagnosis's and author's model calls; the waking RPC's trace has no records and no calls.
- A job with no record keeps a trace of its own and the scope's exit restores the caller's; `continue_trace` outside any scope changes nothing; a nested continuation is undone by its scope's exit.
- Held: `record_grade(held=True)` writes `grade_held` as the grade's child with `grade_held_for_review`, the review reason's code and the unverified criteria; an unheld grade writes none. A live submit whose step holds the grade (`AttemptApplication.grade_held`) writes `grade` then `grade_held` under the submit's trace.
- Replay writes no grade record (a live rebuild leaves `attempt:<id>`'s records as they were).
- A record that cannot be written changes no grade: with `decision_records` dropped, the attempt's score, correctness, FSRS rating, due date, review reason and mastery equal those of an identical vault whose records wrote, and the warning names the trace.
- Failed steps: a failing forecast writer and a failing machine-check sweep each leave a `failed` record with the error class and `tests.test_attempt_chain_records:boom:<line>`, never the planted message.
- The real door: `_probe_contract` refused by value (minutes pinned at 10,000) answers `value_below_minutes_cost` and leaves `probe_value` with the episode and the item's cost; a learner-lane refusal leaves `probe_offer` (`learner_lane`, the causal receipt, `router_decision_id` null), found by `factor:<id>`.
- Prose: a review reason, fallback reason or error type with spaces is `unnamed` or counted, never copied; verdict classes.
- **Fail-first:** the chain read-back with no B7 names (a scratch test, deleted after) on main's `src` (`87ec4085a5`, `git archive`, `pytest -o pythonpath=`): `assert 'grade' in []` fails; on this branch it passes.

**Blindness** (`test_no_grader_context_can_read_decision_records`, `test_a_grading_call_with_records_present_sees_none_of_them`): the grader's prompt builders and context assembly (`attempts.blind_grading`, `grading_support`, `grading`, `ai_contracts`, `grading_admission`, `grader_calibration`) import neither the leaf nor the store directly. ## Storage

From the P-draw branch copies: a `grade` holds about 0.9-1.0 KB of JSON and ids, a `diagnosis` about 0.75 KB, a `job_enqueued` about 0.35 KB, a `probe_offer` about 0.6 KB.
