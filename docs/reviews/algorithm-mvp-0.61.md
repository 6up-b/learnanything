## G3-1: an exposed probe answer is contaminated in its episode

- **Before (mvp-0.60 and below).** The episode observation's contamination test looked only at hints, tutor help and the attempt type. A probe answer given after its feedback was shown ("Show anyway", or feedback released before a wait), after its causes were displayed, or after a reveal in its learning object counted at full weight (`independent_evidence_discount` 1.0) and qualified for completion, while the discriminating observation refused the same answer as not independent.
- **At mvp-0.61.** One exposure test, two callers: `probe_episodes.probe_answer_admissibility` (moved out of `causal_discrimination`, so the episode reader does not import the discrimination module, which put it in an import cycle; it imports `attempts.reveal_ledger` at module level, so the frozen inventory of function-local cross-domain edges loses `causal_discrimination`'s entry and gains none) is what the discriminating observation calls and what the episode calls through `probe_episodes.answer_exposure`. An exposed answer becomes contaminated evidence through the existing machinery (`probe_evidence._presentation_contamination` gains the reason as `answer_exposed`) at the existing value `probe.episode.contaminated_evidence_weight` (0.3 by default; a task share or trace bound already lower stays lower). It never qualifies, so it cannot complete an episode by the breadth, fast-path or action-equivalence rules, nor fill the episode's observation cap. It still moves the posterior, damped: see judgment call 1.
- **Stored history.** `probe_observations` is a raw ledger, so a stored row is never rewritten. `probe_episodes.effective_episode_observations` reads it through the same test at the vault's tag (the held-grade view's pattern), and every reader of the flags that has the vault uses it: `episode_posterior` (posterior and qualifying count; instructional valuation reads that posterior), `probe_completion.evaluate_completion`, `robust_gate_counterfactual`, `_robust_gate_verdict`, `probe_blocks.block_complete`, `calibration_sessions.calibration_session_progress`, and the value receipt's episode-maximum count (`voi_stopping.probe_episode_decision` takes it from its four callers as `qualifying_observations`, through `probe_episodes.qualifying_observation_count`, so the value module does not import the episode reader; that import merged two import cycles). A live observation at the tag is written already contaminated, so live and stored read alike.
- **Left as they were.** The exposure does not change the attempt's activity class (`causal_activity_classification_events`) and does not close the diagnostic segment: a read cannot close a segment, so a live write does not either, and live and stored stay equal. Two readers still count stored flags: the per-session cap (`Repository.qualifying_probe_observation_count_for_session`, which has no vault; only a session open across the upgrade could see an old exposed answer) and the golden-path assessment's baseline projection (`diagnostic_pack._baseline_evidence_projection`, no vault). Both see the right flags for answers recorded at the tag.
- **Who sees what move.** A belief about a cause (the episode posterior, and the misconception marginals persisted from it at the next episode update), a choice of repair (instructional valuation reads that posterior), an episode's completion (an exposed answer no longer counts toward breadth or the cap). On the owner's arxiv vault G3 found three such answers in four.
- **Fail-first.** `tests/test_evidence_reading_successor.py::test_g3_1_an_exposed_probe_answer_is_contaminated_in_its_episode[mvp-0.61-shown_anyway-…]` and `[mvp-0.61-reveal-…]`, and `::test_g3_1_an_exposed_answer_recorded_below_the_tag_reads_contaminated_after_the_upgrade[…]` (both exposures): with `answer_exposure` forced to None (the code before the fix) all four failed; all pass after. The probe answers are model-graded (an approved source; the journeys' self-graded probes write no episode observation) and the post-attempt pipeline writes the discriminating observation, whose refusal reason each test checks.
- **Both tags.** The same test's `[mvp-0.60-…]` arms pin today's result (no contamination, discount 1.0, qualifying). `::test_g3_1_a_clean_probe_answer_is_unchanged_at_the_tag` is the control. The below-the-tag test also checks that, read at the tag, the damped posterior moves each hypothesis no further than the full-weight one did.

## ER-2: an unassessed criterion row is no assessed zero to diagnosis

- **Before.** A row the own-route rule or PF-c marks unassessed was read by trace consistency as a checked, held claim (`consistent_with_claims`) and by the criterion receipt as an assessable, failed criterion.
- **At mvp-0.61.** `causal_attribution._unassessed_criteria` (the tag's gate over `canonical_projection.unassessed_evidence_row`) feeds both readers: trace consistency leaves the criterion out of the awarded scores (a claim about it is `unknown`, `claim_criteria_were_not_assessed`); the criterion receipt gives it no localization outcome (as the canonical projection: a missing dependency reads as passed, so it neither fails nor blocks its descendants) and marks its row not assessable, not passed, no full credit.
- **Who sees what move.** A diagnosis receipt: replay rematerializes it from the stored rows, so the upgrade's rebuild moves the receipts of stored diagnosed attempts with an unassessed criterion (trace-consistency state, protected refs, the criterion list the CLI and feedback show). Rows marked unassessed exist since mvp-0.17 on blind-graded attempts with a divergent route or a PF-c unverified criterion.
- **Fail-first.** `tests/test_causal_trace_consistency_p2.py::test_a_criterion_left_unassessed_neither_holds_nor_fails_a_claim` was a strict xfail; it is now a pair over the two tags. With the gate forced off the `[mvp-0.61]` arm failed on `consistent_with_claims != unknown`.
- **Both tags.** Its `[mvp-0.60]` arm pins today's receipt; `::test_an_assessed_zero_reads_the_same_at_both_tags` is the control.

## K5-11: a replacement grade replays under its own source; a replaced exam answer reaches the exam channel

- **Before.** Replay took its grading source from the feedback row, which records the attempt's first grade and which no regrade rewrites: a model regrade of a self-grade (the startup's deferred regrade) replayed as `self`, so no channel variance and `self` in the grade channel. An exam answer's channel was written by the exam path alone (the dual write skipped every exam type), so a regraded exam answer never reached one.
- **At mvp-0.61** (`src/learnloop/attempts/replay_reading.py`). `replayed_grading_source`: when the attempt has been graded more than once, a model run other than the first grade's wrote the current epoch, and the recorded source is not a model's, the source is that run's (`grading_source_for_run`: `codex` for a provider `CODEX_PROVIDER_NAMES` lists, else `ai`, the regrade door's own split). A recorded model source stands, and so does an adjudicated epoch (no model run wrote it). `replaced_exam_purpose`: the replay of an exam answer with more than one grading epoch writes the replacement on the exam channel's administration (`grade_resolution.EXAM_GRADE_PURPOSE`, assessment, now the one name the exam path uses too), appended once like a practice regrade (the grade channel fixes' `record_replayed_grade`); an exam answer graded once is the exam path's alone, as before.
- **D-55.** The other half of K5-11 (a live observation stamped after the attempt, so re-mints never superseded it) was fixed by `mvp-0.60`'s reuse of the newest observation and is marked so in the ledger; this change does not touch it, and its status cell is left as it is.
- **Who sees what move.** A channel's reliability: a regraded self-grade's raw grade event names its model, so the attempt is read on the model lane (calibrated interpretation, channel variance in the mastery update), which moves mastery and canonical facet cells for its learning object. A regraded exam answer gains an observation, a raw grade event, an interpretation and measurement events, and with them reliable mass on the cells it exercises (in the upgrade test, `recall/procedure_execution` goes from 0 to 0.2435 direct positive mass and certification credit).
- **Fail-first.** `tests/test_evidence_reading_successor.py::test_k5_11_an_ai_regrade_of_a_self_grade_replays_as_the_ai[mvp-0.61]` (`self != ai`) and `::test_k5_11_a_regraded_exam_answer_reaches_the_exam_channel[mvp-0.61]` (`[] !=` the assessment row) failed before the fix and pass after.
- **Both tags.** Their `[mvp-0.60]` arms pin today's `["self", "self"]` and `[]`. Each also rebuilds twice and checks nothing more is appended.

## K5-12: a grader confidence of 0.0 stays 0.0 on replay

- **Before.** Replay read `float(grader_confidence or 1.0)`, so a legal 0.0 (no reliability, live) became 1.0 on any rebuild or regrade replay and was written back to the attempt; at mvp-0.60 the first such rebuild also appended a raw grade event at 1.0 (read as a regrade).
- **At mvp-0.61.** `replay_reading.replayed_grader_confidence` falls back to 1.0 only on a missing value.
- **Who sees what move.** A channel's reliability and what it feeds (mastery noise, the certainty bound) for an answer a model graded at exactly 0.0, on a vault where no replay has run since that grade. A 0.0 that an earlier rebuild or regrade replay already wrote over on the attempt row is not read back (the first raw grade event still holds it); `::test_k5_12_a_zero_an_earlier_rebuild_wrote_over_is_not_restored` pins that. See judgment call 3.
- **Fail-first.** `tests/test_evidence_reading_successor.py::test_k5_12_a_zero_confidence_survives_a_rebuild[mvp-0.61]` failed (`1.0 == 0.0`) before the fix.
- **Both tags.** Its `[mvp-0.60]` arm pins today's 1.0 and the appended event; `::test_k5_12_a_missing_confidence_still_reads_as_full` holds a legacy NULL at 1.0 at both tags.

## K5-3 (the plain part): a certificate probe whose every criterion is unassessed abstains

- **Before.** A verified answer whose every criterion is unassessed carries correctness 1.0 "for scheduling only", and the probe scored it `held`.
- **At mvp-0.61.** The verdict ladder abstains on it after the held-grade rung: `indeterminate`, reason `grade_unavailable` (migration 139's CHECK fixes the vocabulary; a held grade uses the same reason), no success, no correctness (`certification_cold_probe._nothing_assessed`, over `held_grades.pfc_assessments`). The retrieval rule stays as it is (owner, 2026-11-18). `COLD_PROBE_POLICY_VERSION` is `certification_cold_probe_policy_v2`, stamped on the outcome row through `cold_probe_policy_version(config)` (v1 below the tag). See judgment call 2 for the stamps that keep v1.
- **Who sees what move.** A certificate probe's label, and `false_certification_rate` (a CLI report) and the cold-outcome training labels, for probes answered at the tag. The outcome store is append-only and a rebuild never records a probe again, so stored verdicts keep theirs.
- **Fail-first.** With the gate forced off, `tests/test_evidence_reading_successor.py::test_k5_3_a_probe_answer_with_every_criterion_unassessed_abstains[mvp-0.61]` (`held != indeterminate`) and `::test_k5_3_an_assessed_probe_answer_is_scored_at_both_tags[mvp-0.61]` (`v1 != v2`) failed.
- **Both tags.** Their `[mvp-0.60]` arms pin today's `held` verdict, the rate's denominator of 1 and the v1 stamp; the task's scheduling stamp is v1 at both tags.

## Judgment calls (read these first)

1. **What an exposed answer becomes (G3-1).** Contaminated evidence at the existing `probe.episode.contaminated_evidence_weight` (0.3), never qualifying: the ledger row's wording ("moves the posterior by no more than the configured discount") and the brief's (the contamination machinery, an existing value). It cannot complete an episode by any qualifying-count rule, but at weight 0.3 it can still shift the posterior, and so in a near tie the choice of repair; the value-of-information stop also reads that damped posterior, as for any hinted answer. If "must not move the choice of repair" is meant strictly, the existing held-grade shape (`updates_belief` false, discount 0.0) is the one-line alternative in `effective_episode_observations` and the live writer. The exposure also leaves the attempt's activity class and the state segment alone, so that a live write and a read of a stored row agree.
2. **Which stamps say v2 (K5-3).** Only the outcome row, where the verdict is recorded, and only at the tag. Bumping every use of the constant would stamp v2 on vaults below the tag and re-key a measurement opportunity for every learning object at the upgrade (the opportunity's trigger key carries the version); what counts as held out and when a probe is due did not change, so the scheduling stamps keep `certification_cold_probe_policy_v1` (`COLD_PROBE_POLICY_VERSION_V1`). The abstention reason is `grade_unavailable`, the only fitting one migration 139's CHECK allows.
3. **What K5-11 and K5-12 can and cannot repair.** The source rule moves only attempts whose recorded source is `self` or `deterministic` and whose current epoch a different model run wrote; a model regrade of a model grade keeps the first source (a run's provider column can be a default, not a name, as a test fake showed). The run's source comes from its `agent_runs.provider` by the regrade door's own name split, so an AI route served by a Codex-named provider reads `codex`. The exam replacement takes that source too rather than the exam path's fixed `codex`. And a 0.0 confidence an earlier rebuild or regrade replay already overwrote on the attempt row stays 1.0: the fix as specified stops further loss, it does not read the first raw grade event back.

## Interactions

None between the fixes: they share the one predicate and nothing else, and no fix changes another's input. One effect crosses a boundary and is worth reading with K5-11: a regraded exam answer's new channel gives it reliable mass in the canonical projection, which can move a cell's certification credit and so a certificate, and with it what the certificate probe lane schedules.

## The upgrade test

`tests/test_evidence_reading_successor.py::test_the_upgrade_moves_every_case_once_and_a_second_rebuild_moves_nothing`. One synthetic vault at mvp-0.60 (the follow-up journey's: a miss with rival causes and its generated probe) holds every case, written live: two probe answers, one after "Show anyway" and one (a second miss's probe) after a tutor answer on its learning object; a diagnosed miss whose claimed criterion is marked unassessed; a model grade at confidence 0.0 (on a learning object no regrade replays, so the live 0.0 is on the attempt at the upgrade); a self-grade regraded by a model; an exam import regraded by a model; and a certified learning object whose cold probe was answered with every criterion unassessed. Then `learnloop.ops.upgrades.upgrade(to=mvp-0.61)`, then one more rebuild.

- **The upgrade.** Upgraded from mvp-0.60 to mvp-0.61 with its whole-vault-rebuild receipt. Each case moves exactly as its fix says: both exposed answers read contaminated (`answer_exposed` with their reasons, discount 0.3, not qualifying; each episode's qualifying count 1 → 0; the stored rows unchanged); the receipt reads `unknown` and the criterion not assessable; the 0.0 stays 0.0 with one raw grade event; the regraded self-grade gains one raw grade event naming `ai`; the regraded exam answer gains its assessment-channel row; the certificate probe keeps `held` under v1.
- **Derived state moved by the upgrade:** mastery of the learning object both regrades replayed (`lo_svd_definition` logit mean −0.6969 → −0.7074, variance 0.9712 → 0.9744), two new `facet_recall_state` rows (`recall/procedure_execution`, the exam item's cell), that cell's evidence (0 → 0.2435 direct positive mass and certification credit), and the ledgers: +1 activity observation, +2 raw grade events, +2 interpretations, +6 measurement events. No probe observation, discriminating observation or cold-probe outcome is added. Everything else compared is equal.
- **The second rebuild at mvp-0.61** moves nothing: every case's reading, mastery, facet recall, item scheduling state (`due_at`, stability, difficulty), canonical cell evidence and every counted ledger are equal, exactly (no drift seen in these tables; write stamps are not compared).

## What the owner's vaults may show

- Episode posteriors and completion: an episode holding an answer given after "Show anyway", after released feedback, after its causes were shown, or after a reveal: its leading cause less certain (that answer at weight 0.3, not 1.0), one fewer qualifying observation, and a repair choice that can follow. - Certificate probes: no stored verdict changes; new all-unassessed probe answers abstain.
