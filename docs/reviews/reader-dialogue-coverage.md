# Reader dialogue coverage audit (2026-10-12)

**Question (owner, 2026-10-12).** "The original agent dialogue contained a lot more than just the reader workbench — it included things about using natural language queries for the action router's LLM to be able to serve questions from the canonical source to the user as practice questions or as probes, making explorable figures, syntopic readings, and more. Is this included in the spec or were many features from the dialogue cut?"

**Inputs.**
- The dialogue: `docs/brainstorming/Improving Reader's design.md` (1,461 lines). Round 1, the agent's brainstorm: lines 1–508. Round 2, the owner's reply (510–514) and the agent's long answer (517–1086). Round 3, the owner's canonical-source proposal (1088) and the agent's answer (1090–1461).
- The spec: `docs/specs/spec_reader_workbench_v1.md` at `b2041b13` (branch `docs/reader-workbench-spec`).
- Other specs, read on `main` (`fe77a530`, then `19798a0c` after DL1 merged mid-audit; the merge changed only two lines of the daily-loop and learner-field specs): `spec_action_router_v1.md` (§1, §4, §5, §10, §13, §15), `spec_daily_loop_v1.md`, `spec_embedding_consumers_v1.md` (§5.9, §9), `spec_learner_field_v1.md` (§1, §3.1, §4.3), `spec_p3_reader_integration.md` (§1.1, §1.3, §5.4, §7, §8.2), `decision_value_and_commissioning_spec_v1.md` (§6.4), `spec_probe_eig_redesign.md` (§5.9, §7).
- Code on `main` at `19798a0c` (DL1 merged as `mvp-0.14`; the spec was audited at `68213d96`) and on branch `router/r1` at `f789b19d` (not merged; `merge/r1` is being prepared). Two code-mapping agents surveyed it; every `file:line` used below was re-checked by hand.

**Classes.**
- **BUILT:** in a v1 phase as the dialogue proposed it.
- **NARROWED:** in a v1 phase, reduced. The row says what was cut and the stated reason, or "no reason stated".
- **DEFERRED:** listed in the spec's §1, §10 or §17 with a trigger, which the row quotes.
- **ELSEWHERE:** owned by another spec. The row names the section and says whether that spec plans to build it or only mentions it.
- **DROPPED:** not in the spec, or cut with no stated reason.

Each row has one class: the fate of its main idea. Secondary parts are noted in the last column.

| NARROWED | 50 | 43% |

| ELSEWHERE | 10 | 9% |
| DROPPED | 5 | 4% |

Of the 10 ELSEWHERE rows, 8 are built or have a planned phase in their owning spec (rows 10, 11, 29, 31, 71, 76, 77, 114), one is a design rule of router R2's planned build (row 94), and one is only mentioned, never designed (row 95, the router's availability pass). Before TASK 2, row 82 ("one check can't certify a chapter; schedule further checks selectively") was NARROWED by the one-per-session, two-a-day caps; the owner's decision of 2026-10-12 removed them, so it now counts as BUILT.

**The short answer.** Most of round 1 and of the owner's own round-2 asks are in the spec: the workbench, branching inquiry, margin cues, sources-first library answers and the re-entry recap are close to what the dialogue proposed. **Round 3 is mostly not in it, and round 2's learner-model loop is much thinner than proposed.** NARROWED is the largest class (43%): those ideas survive in a reduced form, mostly with a trigger or a Codex-review reason, but 22 of the 50 narrowings state no reason. The Reader builds one learner-initiated retrieval function (`find_sources`) and defers everything in which the router or the tutor queries the sources themselves to router §15.3, a design sketch with no phase row whose trigger (router R2 live, plus T6's hybrid-vs-dense re-evaluation) is at least two merges away. Explorable figures and syntopic reading are in, but narrowed hard: figures to two panel types with no shaded areas, animation or simulation, syntopic reading to a display-only comparison of up to four passages that stores nothing.

**The ten most important cuts, in plain language** (restorations in the last section):

1. **The router can't ask the sources for a question.** The owner's round-3 proposal — the router's LLM searches the textbooks in natural language and serves a real exercise as practice or as a probe, instead of choosing only among existing cards — is deferred to router §15.3 (rows 86, 90, 95, 115). Restoration RS1.
2. **Textbook exercises aren't practice candidates.** `find_practice` searches only existing practice items; a textbook exercise reaches practice only when the learner selects it and imports it. "Don't eliminate cards, change what they represent" — the family keeps the capability, its surface can be the verbatim textbook exercise — is not designed (rows 91, 92, 93, 110). Restoration RS1, step 1.
3. **No search for a question that tells two misconceptions apart.** "Find an exercise that separates boundary-condition confusion from an integration slip" — diagnostic-discrimination retrieval — is in no phase; router §15.3 only sketches it (rows 28, 88, 101). The probe-EIG code that can value such a question exists and, after TASK 2, already values the Reader's follow-ups. Restoration RS1, step 2.
4. **The tutor can't look things up on its own.** It grounds answers on passages near the learner's question (T6, on `main`); in the Reader context it sees only the selection and its neighbours. It never issues its own search, never brings a counterexample or an alternative explanation after one failed, and never compares sources (rows 87, 97, 98). Restoration RS1, step 3.
5. **The workbench never asks a discriminating question mid-problem.** The dialogue's central scenario had LearnLoop notice two plausible explanations of an error and ask one question to separate them before choosing a repair. The workbench localizes the first questionable step and offers hints; diagnosis waits for Submit (rows 18, 39). Restoration RS2.
6. **Reader activity barely updates the learner model.** The dialogue's middle tier of signals — missed predictions, "still unclear" answers, next-step attempts, check verdicts, hints within an unfinished problem — was meant to update candidate misconceptions and routing, never mastery. The spec routes only questions, stuck text and the final submitted attempt (rows 45, 52, 68, 70, 74). See the W1 section: partly a justified constraint, partly an over-cut. Restoration RS3.
7. **Explorable figures lost their flagship example.** The dialogue's two-sided exponential asks the learner to predict "which area represents a given probability"; the pilot has no shaded areas or integral readouts, no PMF bars, no animation, no region-of-a-figure selection, and nothing the tutor can offer (rows 44, 46, 49, 104). Restoration RS5.
8. **Syntopic reading is a one-off table.** No `compare_sources` the tutor can call, no requested perspectives, no stored claims or relations, although the `source_object_relations` proposal layer exists (rows 21, 65, 66, 103). Restoration RS6.
9. **Roles are coarse.** One deterministic ingest role per block; no counterexample, intuition, prerequisite-passage, alternative-treatment or contrast role; the existing `source_objects` layer is unused (rows 100, 102, 109). Restoration RS4.
10. **Retrieval doesn't learn what helps, and cold checks ignore reading exposure.** Which explanation was retrieved and whether it helped is a report of associations that nothing reads back (rows 85, 108); a follow-up can be an exercise whose worked twin the learner just read (row 106). Restorations RS7 and RS8.

**Cuts without a stated reason** cluster in two places: the in-session diagnosis loop (rows 18, 39, 75, 83) and round 3's retrieval intents (rows 87, 88, 89, 96, 97, 98, 102, 106).

## W1 ("reading and help are not evidence") against round 2 §6

**What the dialogue proposed** (lines 879–982). A Reader Observation Compiler turns interactions into typed observations with `authority: routing_only`, and three tiers of signals:
1. **engagement** (read, opened, selected): retrieval and suggestions only, *never mastery*;
2. **formative** (asked, attempted a step, asked for a hint, made a prediction): candidate misconceptions, support policy, placement priorities, and *possibly* low-weight evidence once independently validated;
3. **independent performance**: capability, retention and verified claims.

It also said state should update during the session (line 982), not only at its end, and that the numbers belong to the estimator (917–919).

**What the spec says.** W1: "dwell, highlights, asks, cue taps, figure play, inquiry status, recaps and digests never enter mastery, posterior, certification or FSRS. The only evidence a workbench session produces is its final submitted attempt."

**Where W1 is justified.** For tier 1 and for *evidence* it restates existing invariants and is correct:
- P3 invariant 6 (`spec_p3_reader_integration.md` §1.1): "Reading is not evidence. Dwell, skip, highlight, revisit, 'already know,' and salience projections cannot enter mastery, posterior, or certification projectors." P3 §8.2 adds that the evidence ingestion API rejects every `salience_only` event and that there is "no numeric conversion path from dwell/highlight to correctness, mastery, capability evidence, diagnosis, or certification".
- Learner field F2 (§1) and §3.1's guard: "Signals change routing and predictor state, never evidence, mastery, certification or the demonstrated record." Frictionless S5: asking never lowers the record.
- Learner field F3: anything that changes what is served is an algorithm successor. That is the real reason the spec gives for not adding workbench field signals (§5.6): Phase 6's fast-track serving reads `stuck_confusion` and "help before work".

The dialogue never asked for reader signals to enter mastery. On that point there is no contradiction.

**Where W1 over-cuts.** The dialogue's tier 2 is *not evidence*: it is hypothesis state and routing, which the existing invariants allow and the learner field already does for reader questions (§3.1: "Questions while reading … This is free placement"; routing for new material, live). The spec treats "not evidence" as "not an observation at all" for every new formative signal it creates:
- **Predictions that missed** (RW5), **"still unclear" reconnect answers** (RW1a), **next-step attempts** (RW1b), **"Check my work" verdicts** and **hints taken mid-problem** (RW2b), **cue "not relevant" replies** (RW3) reach nothing but the recap, the report and, for the workbench, the chooser.
- The two that already have a path are kept: reader questions (learner-text gaps, field question signals) and the submitted attempt's `hints_used` / `primed`.
- None of these would need to touch mastery, FSRS or certification to be useful. Two cheap uses stay inside every invariant: (a) "still unclear" text, missed next-step attempts and "doesn't connect" text *name gaps* through the learner-text-gaps pipeline, which already turns learner text into candidate misconceptions and prerequisites; (b) those rows, missed predictions and check verdicts become **inputs to the follow-up valuation** (TASK 2) and to `ReaderActivityV1`, which are routing, not evidence.
- P3 §8.2's bar on a "numeric conversion path" to diagnosis concerns dwell and highlights (salience). Explicit learner answers and learner text are not salience; reader questions already reach learner-text gaps under the current invariants.
- W1's wording also lists "asks" next to "posterior". The learner field's harness arms may use typed questions as weak predictor observations (§3.1 item 2, activated only under §7.4). "Posterior" in P3 invariant 6 means the mastery posterior, but W1 should say so, or it reads as forbidding something another spec plans.

**Verdict.** Justified for evidence and for anything that changes serving without a successor; an over-cut for tier-2 *hypothesis and routing* inputs, which the dialogue proposed, the invariants permit and the field spec already practises. The spec defers one of them with a trigger (row 45) and does not mention the rest. Restoration RS3 below is the smallest fix.

### RS1. Canonical sources as practice, probes and explanations: the smallest path

The owner's vision has three learner-visible parts: (a) textbook exercises served as practice, (b) textbook exercises served as probes that separate rival misconceptions, (c) explanations served from the book. None needs the router's LLM to be the caller. The router as caller is the last step, and it should not hold the others back.

1. **Step 0 — give `find_practice` a caller (S).** Today neither the RPC nor the agent tool has one. DL3's "Practice on…" box and RW4a's "Practice this question" are the same call (spec §18). Router §15.3 itself says to start with the tutor's "Practice this". Lives in RW4a or DL3, whichever lands first. ROI: high; it turns built work into a learner benefit.
2. **Step 1 — textbook exercises as candidates (M).**
   - Add a source-exercise arm to `find_practice`: `search_spans` over the LOs' teaching scope, filtered *positively* to `exercise` and `worked_example` role hints (today they are only excluded) and to blocks not already imported.
   - Return them as a separate candidate kind, `source_exercise`, with source, page and span. They are proposals, not items.
   - Choosing one (a learner pick now; RW6b's valuation or the plan later) runs `import_exercises`. Then the PF-b verifier checks the completed answer before the item can serve as a cold check (row 93). PF's family contract attaches it as a surface of a matching family when one exists (row 91: "change what cards represent").
   - This is RW4b made automatic and the dialogue's implement-first items 1–2. Admission still runs after import.
   - **Where:** a new phase **RW4c** in this spec, which already owns `find_sources(roles=…)` and the source-exercise path. Also lift §17's "Follow-ups from textbook exercises automatically" deferral, so RW6b's uncapped follow-ups can draw on source exercises; that shrinks the `no_item` risk.
   - **ROI:** high. It is the owner's ask, and it reuses T6, `find_practice`, `import_exercises` and PF-b.
3. **Step 2 — typed intents ranked by value of information (S–M).**
   - Add `intent` (`practice` | `diagnostic_probe` | `remediation` | `retention_check`), `target_hypotheses` and `max_estimated_minutes` to `PracticeConstraints`.
   - For `diagnostic_probe`, rank the admitted candidates (items *and* source exercises) with the same adapter RW6b's valuation uses (spec §9.3, TASK 2): `facet_expected_information_gain`, `_misconception_discrimination`, and, for probe instruments, `instrument_expected_information_gain` and `select_discriminating_instrument`.

   - **Where:** RW4c, sharing RW6b's adapter (build it once). **ROI:** high; it is the probe half of the owner's ask.
4. **Step 3 — explanations from the sources for the tutor (M).**
   - Give the tutor's Reader context a span provider, as practice, feedback and library already have. Add an `intent` to `find_sources`: `excerpt` | `explain` | `remediate` | `alternative`.
   - `remediate` passes the open hypothesis's statement and the misconception's `correction_source_span_ids` as `extra_lists` to `search_spans`, which already accepts them. RW3b runs the same per-target retrieval for cues.
   - `alternative` excludes passages already shown in this inquiry, so a second explanation comes from a different source or a different role.
   - In Ask, this is the dialogue's third mode, the personalized bridging explanation (row 98).
   - **Where:** RW4a's `find_sources` plus an Ask-provider change in RW1 (after RW3b). **ROI:** high; the dialogue rated it "even more valuable than retrieving practice problems".
5. **Step 4 — the router as caller (L, router spec).**
   - After R2: a cheap **availability pass** per block candidate (how many admitted candidates exist per intent and facet, with minutes) feeds the planner value (row 95). The chosen block then calls `find_practice(intent=…)`. Retrieved candidates join the logged candidate set, so propensities stay exact (router §15.3's guardrail).
   - **Router spec change:** give §15.3 a phase row (for example R2b). Change its trigger from "T6's re-evaluation settles hybrid vs dense" to "R2 live and RW4c merged": steps 1–3 don't wait on the retrieval-quality question either.
   - **ROI:** medium until R2 has shown block-level gains (router §13).

**Dependencies.** Steps 0–3 need only `main` (T6, `find_practice`, `import_exercises`, PF-b, the EIG functions) plus RW4a.
