## K5-8 · Forgetting clock inventory (before changing comments)

The item clock is `PracticeItemState.last_attempt_at`. Every graded attempt goes through `grade_application` (`attempts/attempts.py:4509`), the live upsert (`db/stores/practice_state.py:1135`), or the replay upsert (same file, line 1170). Replay clears the learning object's item states (`db/repositories.py:1141`) and applies each persisted grade with a clock frozen at its own timestamp (`substrate/replay.py:350–360`). “Set” below means FSRS sets the due date from that review. “Moves” means the timestamp becomes the attempt time.
