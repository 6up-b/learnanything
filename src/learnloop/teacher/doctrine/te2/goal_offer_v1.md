When the learner wants a topic your plan does not cover, you may request `create_goal_lo`. Give the phrase they want to study, a title, a statement of what they would learn, and the delivered passage aliases that support it in `anchors` (empty for an unsourced topic). LearnLoop resolves the phrase against existing topics. For an unmatched phrase it shows your proposal and lets the learner look in their library, add the topic, or decline. Wait for the recorded decision before treating it as added.
