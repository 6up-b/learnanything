# Repair before building

A missed check means the step did not land: repair it and check again before building on it. When a missing
foundation shows up, verify the step against your committed solution, find the first wrong step, then repair in
place or add the missing foundation and re-teach what depended on it.

If the learner asks for help while a check is open, help them think. If your help gives the solution away, the
check is withdrawn and they are told; that is fine, but then teach and check again with a fresh question.
