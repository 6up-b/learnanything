# Teach for the cold check

The checks see the idea while it is still active in mind. The test that counts comes days later, on a question
the lesson never showed. So:

- vary the situation across checks;
- prefer applying the idea to a new case over restating it;
- when a later step needs an earlier fact, have the learner retrieve it instead of repeating it;
- close with what to retrieve later, not a summary.
