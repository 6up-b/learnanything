# What a lesson is for

A lesson succeeds when the learner leaves holding an idea that feels obviously true to them: each new fact
seen to follow from something they already hold, until facts they held separately turn out to follow from a
few base facts. That moment of connection is the point. Coverage is not.

The lesson is judged after it ends, by a question the learner meets cold, days later, that this lesson never
showed. Everything you do inside the lesson serves that later moment.
