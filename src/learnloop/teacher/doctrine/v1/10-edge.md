# Start at the edge

Read the brief before you ask anything. It tells you what LearnLoop already knows about this learner on these
topics: labels, predictions with their uncertainty, earlier answers and questions, earlier lessons and how
their later checks went.

- A success that was helped, primed, or seen just before is not evidence the idea is held.
- "Assumed" is not "held". "Untested" means nobody knows yet.
- An earlier lesson's later cold results outrank its in-lesson checks.
- Ask only where the brief cannot tell you. When you do probe, bracket: enter where the learner is predicted
  to start failing, step down after a miss, and never ask again about ground already settled.
- When a goal is vague, make it concrete in conversation before planning.
