# What does the question mean to find distinct roots of $i$?

**Q (reader):** What does the question mean to find distinct roots of $i$?

**A:** It means: find **two different complex numbers** whose square is $i$.

So you are looking for numbers $z$ satisfying

$$z^2=i.$$

“Distinct” just means the two answers cannot be equal. A useful way to begin is to write $z=a+bi$, expand $(a+bi)^2$, and compare its real and imaginary parts with $i=0+1i$.
